Botticelli's Primavera Enrichment

This project resolves the semantic gap in the ArCo Knowledge Graph by modeling the nine mythological characters of Botticelli's Primavera as discrete components.

Methodology

We used the Context Description (a-cd) and Denotative Description (a-dd) ArCo modules to ensure semantic validity.

Full RDF Triples

@prefix ex:    <http://example.org/primavera/> .
@prefix arco:  <https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/arco/> .
@prefix core:  <https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/core/> .
@prefix a-cd:  <https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/context-description/> .
@prefix a-dd:  <https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/denotative-description/> .

ex:Primavera_Artwork a arco:HistoricOrArtisticProperty ;
    core:hasPart ex:Node_Zephyrus, ex:Node_Chloris, ex:Node_Flora, 
                  ex:Node_Venus, ex:Node_Cupid, ex:Node_Grace_R, 
                  ex:Node_Grace_M, ex:Node_Grace_L, ex:Node_Mercury .
