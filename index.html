KE4H // PRIMAVERA_PROJECT

Project Abstract

This project uses LLMs to enrich the ArCo Knowledge Graph for Botticelli's Primavera by mapping individual mythological components.

Enriched RDF Triples

@prefix ex:    <http://example.org/primavera/> .
@prefix arco:  <https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/arco/> .
@prefix core:  <https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/core/> .
@prefix a-cd:  <https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/context-description/> .
@prefix a-dd:  <https://w3id.org/arco/ontology/denotative-description/> .
@prefix rdfs:  <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .

ex:Primavera_Artwork a arco:HistoricOrArtisticProperty ;
    core:hasPart ex:Node_Zephyrus .

ex:Node_Zephyrus a a-cd:CulturalPropertyComponent ;
    a-cd:hasSubject ex:Subject_Zephyrus ;
    a-dd:technicalCharacteristicNote "Right side of the canvas." .

ex:Subject_Zephyrus a a-cd:Subject ;
    rdfs:label "Zephyrus" .
